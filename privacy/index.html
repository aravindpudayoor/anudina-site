Anudina Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026
Contact: pudayooraravind@gmail.com  ·  Developer: Aravind Sankar Pudayoor

English

Anudina is a personal diary app: daily entries, income and expenses, tasks, and an
optional Malayalam calendar. Your diary is private. This page explains what is
stored, where, and how to remove it.

What Anudina stores

  • On your phone: your diary entries, tasks, transactions, categories and settings. Without an account, this is the only place your data exists. It never leaves your phone.
  • On our server, only if you create an account and sign in:
    • the same diary entries, tasks, transactions and categories (so you can back them up and use more than one phone);
    • your email address and your name (first and last, which you can edit);
    • your password, stored only as a one-way hash;
    • for each phone you sign in on: a hashed sign-in token, the device name your phone reports, and the time it was last used;
    • a short-lived emailed code while you are confirming your email or resetting your password;
    • for each phone that gets reminders from our server: its push address (a Firebase Cloud Messaging token), the phone's time zone and, if you turned it on, the time of the daily "tomorrow's plan" reminder. It is deleted when you sign out of that phone or delete your account.
  • Server logs: like any web server, ours writes technical logs of requests, which include IP addresses. They are used only to find and fix problems, and they rotate automatically. Your diary text, email and password are kept out of them.

What Anudina does not do

  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Reminders reach your phone on time through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. What we send through it is an empty wake-up message: never your diary text or your task titles. The app then shows the reminder from the data on your phone. Google handles the message and the push address under its own privacy policy.
  • No location access. The Malayalam calendar uses the version you choose in Settings, never your location.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone, apart from the two services that work for us: the email-delivery service and Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • We do not read your diary. It is encrypted in transit with HTTPS, but it is not end-to-end encrypted, so the server operator technically could read it. We will not.

Permissions

  • Internet: to sync with the server and receive reminder messages, only after you sign in.
  • Notifications: to show the task reminders and the optional "tomorrow's plan" reminder you set up.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so a task reminder arrives at the minute you chose. You can turn it off in your phone's settings; reminders then still arrive, a few minutes late.
  • Run at startup: so that reminders you scheduled come back after the phone restarts.
  • Vibrate: for reminder notifications.
  • Background work and keeping the phone awake briefly: so a short job, at most every 15 minutes, can check that your reminders are still scheduled and show a missed one late. It runs only on your phone and sends nothing anywhere. On a phone that gets reminders from our server (when you are signed in), this job is not used.
  • Biometrics / screen lock: only for the optional App lock. Your fingerprint or face is handled by Android and never reaches Anudina.

Email

Your email is used to sign in, to send a one-time code that confirms your email
when you create an account, and to send a one-time code if you ask to reset your
password. We send nothing else. The emails are delivered by an email-delivery
service that handles them for us and may not use them for anything else.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete your account: Settings → Sync & backup → Delete account (asks for your password). This permanently deletes your account and everything synced to the server, immediately.
  • Backups: we do not currently keep server backups. If we add them, they will be kept for at most 30 days, and this page will say so.
  • Cannot open the app? Email us from your account address and we will delete the account.
  • Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Export: Settings → Export lets you save your diary, income/expenses and tasks as files at any time.

Children

Anudina is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their
data.

Where data is stored

On a server operated by DigitalOcean in Bangalore, India. Data is protected by
HTTPS in transit and access controls on the server. Reminder wake-up messages pass
through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging, which runs on Google's servers; they
contain no diary text and no task titles.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app and update
the date above.
